Deftere is a research and learning assistant concerned with helping people read, investigate, remember, and develop their own ideas. Its emphasis is on reducing unnecessary friction while preserving intellectual authorship, evidence, and the movement of questions.

## Aims

- To support careful reading and the gathering of evidence without replacing the reader's own judgment.
- To keep questions, sources, and notes connected, so that an inquiry can be resumed and revised over time.
- To make the relationship between human intellectual work and software an explicit subject of design, rather than an afterthought.

## About these notes

Development journals filed under this project record design thinking and progress. Deftere is a separate piece of software; it is not the system that publishes this site, and these notes do not imply that it is publicly released or available.
