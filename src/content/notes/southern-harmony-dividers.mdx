These dividers quote a voice part in William Walker’s *Southern Harmony*. Open one to read the words and find its place in the score. The groupings follow the texts, so you can find a phrase by what it says.

<a href={siteUrl('/notes/shape-note-listening/')}>Hear related recordings by the Word of Mouth Chorus and the singers Alan Lomax recorded</a>.

<a href={siteUrl('/notes/new-harp-of-columbia/')}>Explore the seven-shape dividers from The New Harp of Columbia</a>.

The collection includes 53 interactive dividers. The [score selections](#score-selections) link to 25 facsimiles, including 23 tunes available as scores.
