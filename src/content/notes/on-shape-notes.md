---
title: "On Shape Notes"
description: "A note on the musical typography of this site."
draft: true
---

The small musical figures appearing throughout
this site are drawn from the tradition of
American shape-note singing.

Unlike conventional musical notation, shape-note
systems distinguish pitches through the shapes
of their noteheads.

The visual language of this site takes inspiration
from nineteenth-century tunebooks, particularly
The Sacred Harp.

They are not merely ornaments. They acknowledge
a tradition in which music, communal practice,
religious expression, and printed form remain
closely intertwined.
