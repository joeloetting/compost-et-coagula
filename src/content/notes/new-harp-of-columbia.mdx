These dividers borrow a short phrase from the tenor of each tune in the 1919 printing of M. L. Swan’s *The New Harp of Columbia*. They use Swan’s seven note shapes. Open a divider to read its words and source.

[View the 1919 score at IMSLP](https://imslp.org/wiki/The_New_Harp_of_Columbia_(Swan,_Marcus_Lafayette)).

<a href={siteUrl('/notes/shape-note-listening/')}>Explore the Word of Mouth Chorus and Alan Lomax recordings</a>.

<a href={siteUrl('/notes/southern-harmony-dividers/')}>Explore the four-shape dividers from The Southern Harmony</a>.

The collection includes 50 interactive dividers and 25 additional tunes in the [score selections](#score-selections).
